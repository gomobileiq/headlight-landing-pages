## Subhead
Headlight Route Intelligence is AI-assisted route optimization for businesses that serve the same customers on the same schedule, week after week.

## What it is
Most routing tools plan today's stops. Route Intelligence works on the schedule you already run. It looks across the whole repeating cycle, finds the few changes that save the most miles and hours, and leaves the rest of your operation alone. It is in active development, and the routing engine underneath it is already running in Headlight.

## Benefits
1. **Built for recurring routes.** Water, linen, uniforms, mats, cleaning, medical supply: it optimizes the whole repeating schedule, not just one day.
2. **Change only what pays.** It finds the smallest set of moves with the biggest gain, and you see the result before a single account is touched.
3. **Write rules in plain English.** "Only Sarah services this account." "Never before 9am." AI turns your notes into rules the optimizer follows.
4. **Explainable, not a black box.** Every proposed change comes with the reason, so you can trust it and tune it.

## Who built it
Chris Sciora has built route-planning software for over 30 years. He founded MobileIQ in 1992 and builds Headlight Route Planner, which serves delivery fleets with multi-stop optimization and geospatial logic. He started his career at MapInfo, the desktop-mapping pioneer, and studied computer systems engineering and computer science at RPI.
