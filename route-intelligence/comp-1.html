What it is

Most routing tools plan today’s stops. Route Intelligence works on the schedule you already run. It looks across the whole repeating cycle, finds the few changes that save the most miles and hours, and leaves the rest of your operation alone. It is in active development, and the routing engine underneath it is already running in Headlight.

1

Built for recurring routes

Water, linen, uniforms, mats, cleaning, medical supply: it optimizes the whole repeating schedule, not just one day.

2

Change only what pays

It finds the smallest set of moves with the biggest gain, and you see the result before a single account is touched.

3

Write rules in plain English

“Only Sarah services this account.” “Never before 9am.” AI turns your notes into rules the optimizer follows.

4

Explainable, not a black box

Every proposed change comes with the reason, so you can trust it and tune it.
