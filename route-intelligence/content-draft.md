## Section: Always on, never in the way
It works in the background, around the clock. No screens to check. No buttons to push. No new thing to learn.

## Section: What it quietly watches for
- A new customer added, or one dropped, that nudges a route out of balance.
- A driver who is out, or whose hours change.
- Busy seasons and slow ones that stretch some days and shrink others.
- Small changes over months that slowly pull your routes out of shape.

## Section: What you notice
Nothing. Until something matters. Then you get a short note: what it saw, what it suggests, and why. You decide. Your rules (like "only Sarah services this account") are always respected.

## Section: The promise
A reliable assistant, working every hour, for a little each month. Not a big project. Not a big bill.

## Who built it
Chris Sciora has built route-planning software for over 30 years. He started at MapInfo, a pioneer in desktop mapping, studied computer systems engineering and computer science at RPI, and now builds Headlight Route Planner for delivery fleets. Route Intelligence is the assistant he wishes every dispatcher had.
